## Other systems
- Difficulties: casual, standard, brutal (Brutal adds elites: armored, swift, shielded, regenerating).
- Abilities: Weight (paperweight drop, Q) and Gust (pushes all ink back, E).
- Daily Ink twists: bargain, noglue, swift, glass, elite, purse, pencils, sky, rush.
- Workshop perks (bought with seals): gold, lives, cd, frugal, learn, luck.
- Tower ranks at 25/75/175 kills; gold scraps to tap; music that builds with the threat.
