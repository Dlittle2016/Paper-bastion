# Changelog

## 2026-10-31: More maps, towers and enemies
- Maps: Tri-Fold (`tri`, three entrances), Accordion (`fold`, zigzag, weak flyers), Cross Stitch (`stitch`, two crossing roads).
- Towers: Paper Fan (`fan`, pushes ink back; Cyclone / Windmill), Hangar (`plane`, long-range homing planes; Squadron / Bomber), Hole Punch (`punch`, ignores armor, holes add +10% damage taken each up to 3; Paper Cutter / Three-Hole).
- Enemies: Ink Wasp (`wasp`, fast flying swarm), Streak (`streak`, dashes when hit), Sponge (`sponge`, half damage from blasts), Stamper (`stamp`, stomps and jams towers), The Blot King (`king`, armored boss on waves 27 and 30).
- Tower bar is two rows on phones. Daily Ink picks from all nine maps from 2026-11-01.
- Game source moved into `src/` so updates can be built from the repo.

## 2026-10-09: Daily Ink, Workshop, gold scraps, layered music
- Daily Ink challenge with nine twists and its own daily leaderboard; Workshop perks bought with seals; tappable gold scraps; music that builds with the threat level.

## 2026-10-07: Launch
- Six maps, eight towers, fifteen enemies, three difficulties, tower ranks, boss moments, 8-bit music, online leaderboard.
